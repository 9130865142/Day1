About Us

Cats (Felis catus) are small, domesticated carnivorous mammals known for their companionship, hunting skills, and unique behaviors. General Characteristics Physical Traits: Domestic cats typically weigh between 6 to 15 pounds (2.7 to 7 kg) and stand about 8 to 10 inches (20 to 25 cm) tall at the shoulder. They have retractable claws, sharp teeth, and acute senses, particularly in low light, which makes them excellent hunters. 2 Behavior: Cats are known for their playful and curious nature. They communicate through a variety of vocalizations, including meowing, purring, and hissing, as well as through body language. Cats are also solitary hunters but can be social animals, forming bonds with humans and other pets.
